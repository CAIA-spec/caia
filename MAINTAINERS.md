=========================================================
CAIA MAINTAINERS GROUP (CMG)
MAINTAINERS
=========================================================

This document records the current maintainers of the CAIA specification
and their declared representation status for governance purposes.

Authority is defined by the CMG Charter. This document is a procedural
record only.

---------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
---------------------------------------------------------

This document exists to:
- Identify current CMG maintainers
- Record representation declarations explicitly
- Provide a durable and auditable governance record

Representation is explicit and opt-in. It is never inferred.

---------------------------------------------------------
2. REPRESENTATION RULE (SUMMARY)
---------------------------------------------------------

All maintainers act as individuals by default; organizational representation
occurs only when a maintainer opts in through an explicit public declaration,
at which point organizational seat limits apply.

Employment, funding, or affiliation alone does not imply representation.

---------------------------------------------------------
3. DECLARATION FORMAT
---------------------------------------------------------

Each maintainer must have exactly one of the following declarations:

- "Individual (no organizational representation)"
- "Represents: <Organization Name>"

Declarations:
- Are self-asserted
- May be updated at any time
- Take effect immediately upon update
- Are reviewed for seat balance at term boundaries

---------------------------------------------------------
4. CURRENT MAINTAINERS
---------------------------------------------------------

(Initial entries may be populated during formation.)

Format:
- Name
  Role: Maintainer
  Representation: <Declaration>
  Term: <Start Date> – <End Date or Ongoing>

---------------------------------------------------------
5. RAMP-UP NOTES
---------------------------------------------------------

During early formation, maintainers may all be listed as individuals.
Multiple maintainers employed by the same company do not constitute
organizational representation unless explicitly declared.

Affiliation rules are not applied retroactively.

---------------------------------------------------------
6. UPDATES
---------------------------------------------------------

Changes to this document must be made via committed updates and should
include a brief commit message describing the change.

---------------------------------------------------------
END OF DOCUMENT
