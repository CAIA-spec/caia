=========================================================
CAIA MAINTAINERS GROUP (CMG)
Charter — v1.0
=========================================================

STATUS
------
This charter defines the authority, scope, and operating rules of the
CAIA Maintainers Group (CMG), the sole body responsible for the technical
maintenance and evolution of the CAIA specification.

---------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
---------------------------------------------------------

The CAIA Maintainers Group (CMG) exists to:

- Preserve the technical integrity of the CAIA specification
- Evolve CAIA deliberately and conservatively over time
- Resolve ambiguity in the specification when necessary
- Maintain continuity of stewardship independent of any individual

CMG’s primary obligation is to the long-term clarity, stability, and
architectural coherence of CAIA.

---------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE OF AUTHORITY
---------------------------------------------------------

CMG has exclusive authority to:
- Edit, approve, and publish revisions of the CAIA specification
- Interpret normative (“shall”) language in the specification
- Manage versioning and release cadence
- Accept or reject proposed changes

CMG explicitly does NOT have authority to:
- Certify implementations
- Define interoperability requirements
- Operate test programs
- Issue badges or endorsements
- Govern ecosystem participation
- Represent CAIA commercially or politically

---------------------------------------------------------
3. RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER BODIES
---------------------------------------------------------

CMG is independent of all other organizations.

External bodies may publish observations or recommendations related to
CAIA, but CMG alone determines whether and how such input affects the
specification.

---------------------------------------------------------
4. COMPOSITION
---------------------------------------------------------

Size
----
- CMG shall consist of 3 to 7 maintainers

Eligibility
-----------
Maintainers should demonstrate:
- Deep technical understanding of CAIA
- Architectural judgment
- Willingness to prioritize stability over novelty
- Ability to act independently of employer interests

Employment by a company does not disqualify a maintainer.

---------------------------------------------------------
4.1 REPRESENTATION DECLARATION
---------------------------------------------------------

All maintainers act as individuals by default; organizational representation
occurs only when a maintainer opts in through an explicit public declaration,
at which point organizational seat limits apply.

Representation is never inferred from employment, funding, affiliation,
or contribution history alone.

Maintainers may change their representation status by updating their public
declaration, subject to seat-balance review at the next term boundary.

---------------------------------------------------------
5. SELECTION AND SUCCESSION
---------------------------------------------------------

Initial Formation
-----------------
- Initial maintainers are appointed by the CAIA originator(s)

Ongoing Membership
------------------
- CMG is self-perpetuating
- New maintainers are added by supermajority vote (≥ 2/3)
- Maintainers may resign at any time
- Maintainers may be removed by supermajority vote for:
  - Inactivity
  - Conflict of interest
  - Repeated violation of this charter

Terms
-----
- Maintainers serve fixed, renewable terms (recommended: 18–24 months)
- Terms should be staggered where possible

---------------------------------------------------------
6. DECISION-MAKING
---------------------------------------------------------

General Principle
-----------------
CMG decisions should favor:
- Backward compatibility
- Minimalism
- Clarity over completeness

Voting
------
- Consensus is preferred
- When consensus cannot be reached, a simple majority decides
- Breaking changes require a supermajority (≥ 2/3)

Bias
----
The default bias of CMG is:
“Reject unless clearly justified.”

---------------------------------------------------------
7. CHANGE PROCESS
---------------------------------------------------------

Changes may be proposed via:
- Pull requests
- Issue submissions
- Formal change proposals

CMG evaluates proposals based on:
- Alignment with CAIA core principles
- Evidence from real-world use
- Impact on existing implementations
- Risk of unintended coupling or scope creep

---------------------------------------------------------
8. TRANSPARENCY
---------------------------------------------------------

CMG shall:
- Conduct discussions in public where practical
- Publish rationale for accepted or rejected changes
- Clearly document breaking changes

---------------------------------------------------------
9. FAILURE AND DISSOLUTION
---------------------------------------------------------

If CMG becomes inactive:
- The last published CAIA specification remains valid
- No external body automatically inherits authority

If CMG dissolves intentionally:
- Custody of the CAIA specification shall be transferred
  to a successor group explicitly designated by CMG
- Absent such designation, the specification remains frozen

---------------------------------------------------------
10. AMENDMENTS
---------------------------------------------------------

This charter may be amended only by a supermajority of CMG maintainers.

=========================================================
END OF CHARTER
